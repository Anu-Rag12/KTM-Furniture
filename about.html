About KTM Furniture

Who We Are

KTM Furniture is a local furniture manufacturer and seller based in the Kathmandu Valley (Narephant / Balkumari area, Lalitpur).

We specialize in premium wooden furniture — especially Sisau wood sofa sets — with high-density foam, durable PU paint finishing, and full customization options.

Our goal is simple: deliver good quality furniture at fair prices, made right here in Nepal, with honest service.
